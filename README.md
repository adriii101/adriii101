# Adrián Esteban Romero

### Political Scientist · Data Analysis · Computational Social Science

I work at the intersection of **social research, political behaviour and computational methods**, with a particular interest in affective polarization, political communication and digital social research.

My work combines traditional social science methodology with data analysis, visualization and computational approaches to study complex political and social phenomena.

---

## Featured project

### Affective Polarization in Spain

Interactive research application developed in **R Shiny** to explore political, relational and affective factors associated with extreme affective polarization.

The project combines data preparation, binary logistic regression, model comparison, diagnostic statistics and interactive visualization.

**R · Shiny · Logistic Regression · Data Visualization · Computational Social Science**

[View repository](https://github.com/adriii101/App-Polarizaci-n-Afectiva) · [Live application](http://adriesteban11.shinyapps.io/app-r-tfm-polarizacion)
